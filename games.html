The Game

WELCOME TO THE NIGHT SHIFT.

You have been assigned to the overnight shift at Atlas Robotics & Distribution — Facility 07. Your job sounds simple: monitor the facility, manage the power and make it through the night.


Unfortunately, the facility isn't as empty as it should be.

Gameplay

SURVIVE THE FACILITY.

01

CCTV SYSTEM

Monitor different areas of Facility 07 through the security camera network.

02

POWER MANAGEMENT

Doors, lights and security systems consume power. Running out isn't an option.

03

ANIMATRONICS

Something is moving through the facility. Keep track of where it is.

04

SURVIVE UNTIL 6 AM

The shift begins at midnight. Make it to 6 AM.
